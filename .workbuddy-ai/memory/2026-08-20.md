## Customer App — touch lag / performance fix
- Complaint: proqram ləng işləyir, touch gecikir.
- Root cause: `.customer-app-aurora` (and `.web-aurora-subtle`) had `filter: blur(60px)`/`blur(80px)` on a **full-screen fixed layer** that is animated (`custAuroraDrift`, transform-only but re-rasterizes the blurred texture every frame) → constant mobile GPU/CPU cost = touch latency. Plus `.customer-app-noise` used `mix-blend-mode: overlay` over the full viewport.
- Fix: removed the `filter: blur(...)` from both aurora layers (radial gradients already fade to transparent, look preserved) and removed `mix-blend-mode: overlay` from the noise layer. Kept the drift animation (transform/opacity = cheap on GPU).
- `CustomerApp.tsx:434` `refreshOrders` setInterval(8000) still re-renders whole tree every 8s (periodic, not constant) — left as-is; revisit only if lag persists after blur removal. Fortune interval at 1080 is self-clearing, not a concern.
- `vite build` (customer) → success. Rule: avoid full-screen `filter: blur()` + animation on the customer app (mobile killer).
