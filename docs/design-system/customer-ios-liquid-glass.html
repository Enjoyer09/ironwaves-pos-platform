What the world is doing.

Four research sources distilled into the decisions this concept is built on.

01 / RESEARCH

1 · Apple Liquid Glass

Apple's iOS 26 language is not "more blur" — it is a hierarchy system.

  • ✓Core rule: glass belongs on toolbars, tab bars and controls — never covering the content itself.
  • ✓Five traits: translucency, refraction (lensing), specular highlights, background-adaptive color, state morphing.
  • ✕Never mix regular glass and clear glass in the same view; never stack glass on glass.
  • ✓System honors Reduce Transparency / Increase Contrast / Reduce Motion by default.
developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materialsiOS 26 Liquid Glass practice guide (2026)

2 · 2026 glassmorphism practice

"Volumetric glass" matured from a trend into a token-driven system.

  • ✓Blur tokens, not vibes: 4px context → 16px bars → 24px sheets → 40px modal isolation.
  • ✓Specular edge: 0.5–1px light stroke defines the glass "thickness".
  • ✕Three horsemen of failure: low contrast, text over busy backgrounds, distracting layers.
  • ✓Semantic border: ≥4.5:1 against both the glass fill and what's underneath.
Timothy Graf — Mastering Glassmorphism UX in 2026

3 · Who uses it well

Glass now dominates fintech, SaaS dashboards and premium consumer apps.

  • ✓Fintech (Revolut-class): floating glass balance cards and controls over vivid gradients.
  • ✓SaaS (Linear/Vercel-class): frosted rails and command palettes over dark canvases.
  • ✓Consumer/loyalty: glass reserved for scan buttons, tab bars, filters — content rows stay solid.
  • ✓Reported effect: +42% visual recall, +31% perceived quality (2026 industry study).
Glassmorphism: The Definitive Guide (2026)

4 · Accessibility is the gate

Glass without fallbacks is a usability bug, not a style.

  • ✓Reduce Transparency ON → glass solidifies to opaque high-contrast surfaces instantly.
  • ✓Text on glass must clear 4.5:1 against the worst-case background beneath.
  • ✓Reduce Motion → morphing and refraction animations switch off.
  • ✓Try the toggle in the top bar — every glass surface here has an opaque twin.

Rules that keep it honest.

Every do/don't below comes straight from the research — this is the review checklist for the build.

04 / RULES & A11Y

Do

  • ✓Glass only on navigation & controls: tab bar, cart bar, search, chips, add buttons, avatar, sheets.
  • ✓Content stays solid: loyalty card, product rows, settings groups — opaque ≥88% with real shadows.
  • ✓One vivid background behind glass (warm gradients) so refraction has something to bend.
  • ✓Specular edge on every glass surface — the 1px light stroke is what makes it read as "thick".
  • ✓Fallback layer: Reduce Transparency swaps every glass surface to an opaque, bordered twin.
  • ✓48px minimum targets, visible focus ring, reduced-motion honored (carried from v2).

Don't

  • ✕No glass content cards — list bodies and long text on blur is the #1 readability failure.
  • ✕No glass-on-glass stacking (a chip inside a glass card inside a glass sheet).
  • ✕No mixed glass opacities in one view (regular + clear side by side breaks hierarchy).
  • ✕No small text directly on the gradient — always a glass or solid surface beneath.
  • ✕No unbounded blur — blur amount comes from the token scale, never ad-hoc per card.
  • ✕No decoration loops — motion only for state changes; stop under Reduce Motion.

05 / HANDOFF

Integration path

Scoped, gradual, reversible. Customer root only — POS/admin tokens never change.

1. New src/theme/glass-tokens.css (scoped
   .customer-design-system + data-customer-theme)
2. Glass components: TabBar, SearchBar,
   ChipRow, CartBar, Sheet, IconButton
3. Solid restyle: loyalty card, product rows,
   settings groups (keep v2 type/spacing)
4. prefers-reduced-transparency fallback
   wired to the same tokens
5. Migrate screen by screen: Home → Menu →
   Offers → Profile → Auth

Status: design proposal only. Nothing is imported by the live app; the Quiet UI v2 preview remains available until you choose one.

Open decisions

  • ?Dark default? Glass reads strongest on the espresso canvas — propose system-preference with warm-dark default.
  • ?Vividness of background: shown at high saturation; can be toned down per screen if content density grows.
  • ?iOS 26 only? Native glassEffect() needs iOS 26; the CSS layer degrades to frosted blur on iOS 18 — decide minimum OS for the app.
  • ?Scope: customer app confirmed; POS/admin restyle can reuse the same token file later.

Say the word and I start phase 1 (tokens + TabBar + Home) with tests, build and on-device verification as usual.
