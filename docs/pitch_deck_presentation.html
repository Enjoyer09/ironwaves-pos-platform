⚠️ The Problem

Hospitality Tech is Fragmented, Slow & Expensive

Merchants are crippled by tool sprawl, hardware lock-in, and lost revenue during peak hours.
🧩 Tool Fragmentation

Restaurants juggle 4–6 disconnected vendors: POS register, kitchen tickets, loyalty club, mobile floor plan, and accounting.

Cost: $400–$900/month/location with frequent data sync failures.

🐢 Crippling Checkout Lag

Legacy systems lag 2–4 seconds per item tap or screen transition. Slow checkouts create long queues and customer walk-outs.

Impact: 15–25% lost revenue during peak dining rushes.

🔒 Hardware Lock-In

Legacy vendors force merchants into proprietary $2,000–$5,000 terminals with zero cross-platform flexibility.

Impact: Prohibitive capital expenses for multi-branch expansion.

💡 The Solution

The Unified Cloud-Native Operating System

One integrated platform replacing multiple legacy tools with blazing speed.
  • Sub-100ms Touch Register: Instant cart actions, barcode scanning, multi-tender split payments (Cash, Card, Points, Staff).
  • Smart Multi-Station KDS: Real-time WebSocket ticket routing to Bar, Hot Line, and Cold Prep with auditory rush alerts.
  • Waiter Mobile Pad: 1-hand mobile table ordering, seat check splitting, and course firing right at the dining table.
  • Apple iOS V3 Loyalty PWA: Digital member passes with fraud-proof dynamic QR tokens and Starbucks-style store selection.
  • Fiscal Shift Integrity: Automated X-Reports and end-of-day Z-Reports with precise double-entry cash drawer reconciliation.
